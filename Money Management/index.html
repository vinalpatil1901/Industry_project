What is a SIP?

A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) means investing a fixed amount of money every month in a mutual fund.

  • You do not need to research and select individual investments yourself; professional fund managers manage the mutual fund.
  • Over time, your money can grow through compounding, where returns may also start earning returns.
  • SIPs in mutual funds reduce the need for individual investment decisions, but they still carry market risk.
